# Phase 2 – Researching and Planning

## Research

Research shows that bushfires in Australia have become increasingly destructive in recent years. According to climate scientists, rising temperatures and prolonged drought conditions increase the likelihood of severe bushfires. The 2019–2020 Black Summer bushfires caused widespread environmental and economic damage across Australia.

Useful Sources:
- Kaggle dataset
- NSW Government Data
- Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS)

## SEE-I Paragraph

Bushfires in Australia are becoming more severe due to changing climate conditions. Higher temperatures and dry weather make it easier for fires to spread quickly. For example, the Black Summer bushfires burned millions of hectares of land and destroyed many homes and habitats. This can be compared to dry paper catching fire faster than wet paper because dry environments increase fire intensity.
